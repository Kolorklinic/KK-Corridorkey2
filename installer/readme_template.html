CorridorKey 2 for Resolve

Version @VERSION@, @DATE@ · by KolorKlinic, based on the OFX by Creative Video Tips; model by Corridor Digital

CorridorKey 2 for Resolve is an OpenFX node that keys green and blue screens with CorridorKey 2.0, Corridor Digital's AI keyer, running on your Mac's own GPU. It works on DaVinci Resolve's Fusion and Edit pages and in Fusion Studio. Hair, fur, motion blur and uneven screens come out cleanly without keyer settings, and a rough garbage matte on its Hint input removes anything outside the screen.

What you need

An Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or later) with 64 GB of memory or more, and about 7 GB of free disk for the model, plus roughly 2.5 MB for each processed HD frame. An M5 Max keys an HD frame in about a second; an M1 Max takes about five.

After installing

  1. Restart Resolve or Fusion Studio.
  2. Add Effects > OpenFX > KolorKlinic > CorridorKey 2 to a clip.
  3. In the node's Setup section, press Install Model. Terminal opens, asks you to accept the CorridorKey licence, then downloads CorridorKey 2.0 and its weights (about 5 GB) into your home folder. This happens once per Mac.
  4. Press Process. A progress window shows how far along it is.
